# GETON Kenya — Security Policy

## Reporting a Vulnerability

If you've discovered a security vulnerability in GETON, please report it
privately so we can fix it before public disclosure.

### How to report

**Email:** security@geton.co.ke (PGP key: https://geton.co.ke/pgp-key.txt)
**Web form:** https://geton.co.ke/security/report
**Phone:** +254 709 123 457 (ask for the Security Team)

### What to include

- A clear description of the vulnerability
- Steps to reproduce
- Affected URLs/parameters
- Screenshots or proof-of-concept (if available)
- Your name (for credit) — or anonymous if you prefer

### Our commitment

- We will acknowledge your report within **24 hours**
- We will provide a timeline for a fix within **72 hours**
- We will credit you in our acknowledgments page (if you wish)
- We will notify you when the issue is fixed
- We follow **coordinated disclosure** — please give us 90 days before public disclosure

### Bug Bounty

We run a paid bug bounty program. Rewards range from KSh 5,000 to KSh 500,000
depending on severity. See https://geton.co.ke/bug-bounty for details.

### Out of scope

- Denial of service (DoS) attacks
- Social engineering of our staff
- Physical attacks on our offices
- Self-XSS or clickjacking on unauthenticated pages
- Reports from automated scanners without PoC
- Issues in third-party services we use (Paystack, M-Pesa)

### Safe harbor

We will not pursue legal action against researchers who:
- Make a good-faith effort to avoid privacy violations
- Only access data necessary to demonstrate the vulnerability
- Do not exploit the vulnerability beyond what's needed to prove it
- Report it to us first and allow reasonable time to fix

Thank you for helping keep GETON and our 2 million+ users safe.
